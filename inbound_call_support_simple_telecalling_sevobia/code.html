Frequently Asked Questions

Plain, straightforward answers without any technical confusion.

How do I forward my phone calls to Sevobia? help_outline

It takes just 30 seconds on your regular phone! We give you a dedicated 10-digit number. On Jio or Airtel, dial *21*[Sevobia Number]# to forward all calls, or *67*[Sevobia Number]# to forward only when you are busy or decline a call.

Can callers speak both Hindi and English fluently? translate

Yes, 100%. All our callers are fluent in clean Hindi and professional English. We also have regional language callers available on request (Kannada, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali).

What if a caller asks a question that is not in the script? chat_bubble

Our callers never guess or give wrong information. They politely state: “Let me check with the team lead and have someone get back to you with the exact answer within 15 minutes.” You get an alert right away with the customer’s question.

Can I listen to call recordings to verify caller quality? graphic_eq

Yes, every single inbound call is securely recorded and logged. You can click on the WhatsApp link to listen to the audio right on your phone, anytime you wish.
