Frequently Asked Questions

Simple, direct answers with no confusing corporate jargon.

Will you answer using my exact business name?

Yes, absolutely. To your customers, our executives appear as your internal team members. They greet with your company name, follow your established etiquette, and adhere to your exact guidelines.

What if a customer writes in Hindi, Hinglish, or regional terms?

All our agents are based in India and are fluent in conversational English, Hindi, and everyday Hinglish phrasing. If a buyer sends a voice note in Hindi or writes phonetically, our team responds naturally in the same tone.

Do I need to buy expensive software or WhatsApp API tools?

No. If you already have an official WhatsApp Business setup, we simply link with it. If you do not have one, we guide you through standard verification or connect through simple multi-device access without any hidden software bills.

How do I know what was spoken with my customers?

Every chat remains 100% visible to you in real-time on your master phone or CRM dashboard. Additionally, our team sends an evening report summarizing key conversations, leads, and payment confirmations.

Is there any long-term contract lock-in?

None at all. You can start with a 2-day trial, continue on a simple monthly arrangement, and pause or upgrade anytime your business requirements shift.
