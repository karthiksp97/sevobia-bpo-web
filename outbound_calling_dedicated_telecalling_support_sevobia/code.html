Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers without confusing technical terms.

How do I give you my phone numbers or leads? expand_more
You can simply send us an Excel or Google Sheet with names and phone numbers. Or, if you run Facebook or Google Ads, our team can connect directly to your forms so we get notified the second a lead signs up.
What if the customer speaks Hindi or a regional language? expand_more
All our calling specialists speak fluent, polite Hindi and clear English. In addition, we have dedicated callers fluent in Kannada, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali upon request for regional campaigns.
Can I listen to how your callers talk to my customers? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. Every single outbound call is recorded for quality. In your evening WhatsApp or Excel summary, you can click on any lead row to listen to the call audio and hear their exact conversation.
What if a lead asks a technical or unusual question? expand_more
Our callers will never make up false promises. If someone asks a deep technical or uncommon question, our caller politely responds: “Let me verify this with our senior specialist and message you on WhatsApp within 15 minutes.” This protects your reputation while keeping the lead warm.
Is there any long-term contract or lock-in? expand_more
No, our services are month-to-month. You can scale up the number of callers, pause during off-season, or stop anytime with simple notice. There are no lock-in contracts or surprise cancellation charges.
