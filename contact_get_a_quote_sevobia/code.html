How do you calculate our quote without knowing our exact call volume?

We start with your estimated daily inquiries and match you to a flexible bracket. You are never locked into an inflexible minimum. As your business experiences busy festival seasons or quiet months, you can easily adjust your agent hours with 7 days notice.

help

Do we need to buy any new software or SIM cards?

No. Our systems connect seamlessly with your existing mobile numbers and landlines via standard call forwarding. When a patient or customer dials your usual number, it rings securely at our dedicated agent pod.

help

Can we try a sample call before signing up?

Yes. In fact, we insist on it. We prepare a 2-minute sample script with your business name and common customer scenarios so you can test caller tone, fluency, and politeness firsthand before making any commitment.

Do you provide proper GST tax invoices for business expense write-offs?

Yes, absolutely. We are a registered Indian private limited entity. We provide 100% compliant monthly tax invoices detailing our GSTIN and your GSTIN so you can claim full input tax credit without paperwork headaches.
