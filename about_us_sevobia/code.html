Why Sevobia Exists

Everyday Indian businesses lose up to 40% of their new leads and customer inquiries simply because owners are busy in client meetings, traveling, checking stock, or managing workers.

When an interested client calls and gets an unanswered ring or busy tone, they immediately call a competitor. Yet, hiring an in-house receptionist or telecaller introduces costly office rent, desktop computers, sick leaves, repeated training, and everyday management stress.

Sevobia was built to give every doctor, factory owner, builder, wholesaler, and retail founder a polite, professional front-office team without the cost or burden of running an internal call desk.
