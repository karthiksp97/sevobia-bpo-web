## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the operational rigor, high-trust stability, and dynamic agility demanded by modern enterprise business process outsourcing and omnichannel customer support. It balances corporate authority with human-centered responsiveness, projecting enterprise-scale security alongside energetic problem-solving capability.

The target audience comprises enterprise VP-level operators, procurement directors, scaling tech founders, and CX leaders seeking turnkey team augmentation. The visual mood avoids generic call-center tropes, instead channeling the crisp, data-driven sophistication of modern high-tier SaaS platforms and global management consultancies.

The overall aesthetic integrates **Corporate Modernism** with high-conversion product marketing principles:
- Grounded, authoritative canvas foundations anchored by deep navy structure.
- High-intent conversions driven by saturated radiant orange focal points.
- Precision structural framing with razor-sharp 1px border metrics, subtle depth separation, and ample breathing room.
- Micro-interactions characterized by low-latency, friction-free transitions that evoke operational competence.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the dual nature of high-performance BPO: rock-solid infrastructure paired with human dynamism.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#0B192C`):** Represents institutional permanence, enterprise data confidentiality, and governance. Serves as the primary structural hue for main headers, dark hero containers, and dominant navigation blocks.
- **Secondary Vivid Orange (`#FF6A00`):** Represents responsiveness, warm human connection, and high-velocity execution. Dedicated exclusively to primary calls-to-action, key performance highlights, interactive active states, and critical notification accents.
- **Tertiary Slate Blue (`#1E3E62`):** Acts as an intermediate bridge for supporting navigation layers, table headers, active secondary chips, and hover states for dark surfaces.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Base White (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated card surfaces, inputs, and primary content canvas.
  - Off-White Canvas (`#F8FAFC`): Background wash providing contrast against pure white cards.
  - Border Gray (`#E2E8F0`): Subtle framing for data tables, card perimeters, and input boundaries.
  - Subdued Text (`#64748B`): Secondary labels, meta descriptions, and timestamps.
  - Body Text (`#1E293B`): High-contrast, fatigue-reducing charcoal for dense analytical text and support logs.

### Application Rules
- Never use the orange secondary hue for large background fills; reserve it strictly for directional focal points to preserve conversion power.
- Dark navy sections must be balanced with minimum 16:1 text contrast using pure white and muted ice blue (`#94A3B8`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent, energetic display headlines with **Inter** for sustained technical clarity in high-density enterprise environments.

### Usage Guidance
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides welcoming curves balanced by rigid geometric alignment, mirroring the circular geometry in the brand’s visual identity. Use for hero headers, section milestones, card headlines, and metric values.
- **Inter:** Provides maximum legibility for dense client SLA dashboards, agent ticket views, long-form service descriptions, and form inputs.
- **Micro-labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to tag category badges, SLA status indicators, and sub-headers above major titles.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an **8-point base spatial system** integrated within a responsive 12-column grid to maintain clean visual rhythm across data density and marketing layouts.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, max content constraint of 1280px, 32px gutters (`gutter-lg`), 80px section margins (`margin-lg`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, 24px gutters (`gutter`), 48px outer canvas margin (`margin-md`). Multi-card grids reflow to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters (`gutter-sm`), 24px outer margins (`margin`). Hero metrics and feature matrices stack vertically into single-column layouts.

### Structural Flow
- Interactive modules, form groups, and list sequences utilize strictly proportional internal spacing (`space-sm` for closely related siblings, `space-md` between input stacks, and `space-xl` between modular sections).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through **low-contrast architectural outlines** blended with **tinted ambient shadows**. Rather than relying on generic black alpha shadows, surfaces cast ultra-soft, cool navy undertones (`#0B192C`) that maintain brand coherence and avoid visual mud.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Used for base surfaces (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow; separation occurs purely via structural 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Default state for metrics panels, service offerings, and data cards. Crisp 1px border in `#E2E8F0` layered with `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Float):** Triggered when hovering over interactive cards, quick-action drawers, or active filters. Border shifts to `#CBD5E1` with shadow expanding to `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.09)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Sticky conversion ribbons, enterprise quote modals, and slide-overs. Shadow elevated to `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.16)`.
- **Accent Glow:** Primary interactive CTA hover states receive a soft directional glow: `0 4px 14px 0 rgba(255, 106, 0, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape philosophy. This choice preserves crisp enterprise authority while softening operational friction through accessible, rounded touchpoints.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Small Controls (`rounded` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Primary form fields, buttons, dropdown triggers, and status badges.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Analytical cards, testimonial clusters, pricing tiers, and modal panels.
- **Hero & Media Containers (`rounded-xl` = 1.5rem / 24px):** Product feature previews, embedded case studies, and dashboard visual frames.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for live status chips (e.g., "Available 24/7", "99.8% CSAT"), micro-tags, and avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion):** Background in Vibrant Orange (`#FF6A00`), label in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, height 44px (desktop) / 48px (mobile). Hover transforms to `#E65F00` with subtle scale-up and orange accent glow.
- **Secondary (Corporate Navy):** Background in Deep Navy (`#0B192C`), label in pure white (`#FFFFFF`). Used for portal logins, documentation downloads, and secondary operations.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** White background with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), label in Navy (`#0B192C`). Hover fills to `#F1F5F9`.

### Input Fields & Forms
- **Standard Input:** Height 44px, 8px radius, white surface, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), text in `#1E293B`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border shifts to `#0B192C` with a 3px ring in `rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.1)`. No harsh primary orange outlines on inputs, keeping the workflow grounded and focused.
- **Error State:** Border shifts to Crimson (`#EF4444`) with descriptive micro-copy rendered below the field in 12px.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with 16px corner radius and a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`.
- Includes a 4px top accent border option in Vibrant Orange (`#FF6A00`) to indicate recommended tiers, active outsourcing plans, or featured enterprise solutions.
- Padding scaled at 24px (`space-lg`) on mobile, expanding to 32px on desktop.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators (e.g., "SLA Met", "Tier-1 Certified"):** Pill-shaped, 4px vertical padding, 12px horizontal padding.
- Neutral active chips use light navy background (`#F1F5F9`) with Navy text (`#0B192C`).
- Live highlights use 10% tint of orange (`rgba(255, 106, 0, 0.1)`) with vivid orange text (`#D95A00`) for accessibility.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px x 20px controls with 4px corner radius for checkboxes and full circle for radio buttons.
- Inactive: 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`.
- Checked: Background filled with Navy (`#0B192C`) with a crisp white check icon; key transactional opt-ins (such as contract agreements) highlight with Orange (`#FF6A00`).

### B2B Specific Components
- **Metric Highlight Tiles:** Large numerical readout in `display-hero` (Navy), paired with a 12px uppercase label and an micro-trend badge indicating performance growth.
- **SLA Progress Trackers:** Continuous 6px track in `#E2E8F0` with filled progress in `#FF6A00` or `#1E3E62` indicating real-time response rate fulfillment.
- **Agent Pod Cards:** Dedicated micro-profiles highlighting dedicated agent headcount, languages spoken, and domain certifications with subtle status indicator dots.
